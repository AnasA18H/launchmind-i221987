Discover and buy unique, locally-made products

Support local artisans and small makers in your area

Features

  • Listing Creation

    Allow artisans to easily create and manage listings for their products

  • In-App Chat

    Enable buyers to communicate with artisans directly through the app

  • Pickup Scheduling

    Allow buyers to schedule pickup times with artisans

  • Discovery Feed

    Display a feed of nearby listings to buyers, curated by location and category

  • User Reviews and Ratings

    Allow buyers to leave reviews and ratings for artisans and their products
